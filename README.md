DeMail – Privacy Policy
Last updated: Nov 5, 2026
1. Introduction
DeMail is an Android email app published by deapps.studio (Vršac, Serbia) that lets you read and manage your Gmail inbox. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: DeMail talks directly to Google on your device, and the developer does not collect, receive or store your emails or personal data.
2. Information DeMail accesses
When you sign in with Google, DeMail asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify. It is needed to show your inbox and to let you change the state of your messages. Through it the app accesses:
•	Your Gmail address, to show which account is signed in.
•	Message details for the newest inbox messages: sender, subject, a short text preview (snippet), date and time, and labels such as Inbox and Unread.
•	Label changes you request: archive, mark as spam, move to trash, and mark as read or unread.
DeMail does not read attachments, does not access your contacts, and does not send emails on your behalf. It collects no other personal data such as your location, device identifiers or contacts.
3. How the information is used and stored
The information above is used only to provide the app's features: showing your inbox and applying the actions you choose. Requests go directly from your phone to Google's Gmail API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. DeMail has no servers of its own, so your emails never pass through the developer.
On your device, the app keeps your emails in memory while it is running and a single "signed in" setting so it can sign you in again at the next launch. The access token issued by Google is held in memory only and is not written to storage.
4. Sharing, advertising and analytics
DeMail does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers, and your email content is never used for advertising, profiling or to train any AI or machine-learning model.
DeMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described in this policy, and no human reads your email data.
5. Security, retention and your choices
All communication with Google uses HTTPS, and sign-in is handled by Google's own authorization screen, so DeMail never sees your Google password. Because the developer stores nothing, there is no developer-held copy of your data to retain or delete.
You are in control:
•	Sign out: choose Sign out in the app menu. This clears the emails and account details held in the app.
•	Revoke access: remove DeMail at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party access.
•	Uninstall: removing the app deletes everything it stored on your device.
Actions you take in DeMail (archive, spam, delete, read/unread) are applied to your Gmail account and can also be undone in Gmail.
6. Children, changes and contact
DeMail is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.
